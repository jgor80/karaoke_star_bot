      How points work

      • 10 for every night you sing (at any bar)
      • 3 per song, up to 4 songs a night
      • Up to 5 more per song for the applause (half the crowd meter score)
      • Up to 5 more per song for hype reactions from phones (half a point each)
      • 15 bonus for each extra Oke Night bar you sing at in the month

      Duets count for everyone in them. Hosts don’t compete. Same name, same person, so sign up the same way every time.
