How a bar gets set up

  1. Your bar gets its own pageokenight.com/yourbar, with your night’s schedule and your host’s name.
  2. The host installs the helperIt runs quietly on the karaoke laptop and sends each song to your page.
  3. Put the code on the tables and the TVGuests scan it and play along. That’s it.

What it takes

  • A Windows laptop running your karaoke app. Oke Night works with one popular karaoke app for now. Tell us which one you use in the form and we’ll let you know if it fits.
  • A TV or screen for the scan code and the big-screen recap (optional, but the crowd loves it).
  • Wifi for the laptop. Guests use their own phones. No app to install, no sign-ups.
  • $20 a month for the bar. Always free for singers. Send the form below and we’ll get you set up.

The owner gets the numbers from each night (songs, singers, first-timers, busiest hour, applause) the next morning. No names or song titles. See a sample report (real numbers from one night, with the bar’s name left out).
