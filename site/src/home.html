What the crowd gets

Everything updates live as songs start, so the whole room follows along between turns.

🎙️

Who’s on the mic

The song playing now, who’s up next and roughly how long the wait is.

🔥

Live reactions

Tap fire, mic drop or tomato on whoever is singing. The crowd’s taps decide some of the awards.

🏆

Awards and badges

MVP, power ballad, crowd favorite and dozens of badges, handed out every night.

🎯

Karaoke bingo

A fresh card each night that fills itself in as the songs roll.

📝

Crowd draft

Draft a friend to sing a song, and the room votes up the picks it wants most.

📖

Songbook

Search the song list by genre, era or duet, save the ones you want and request what’s missing.

🤫

Secret songs

The host hides songs with prizes. Sing one and the whole bar finds out.

📺

Big screen recap

Awards, top singers and the night’s highlights on the bar’s TV.

For bars and hosts

⏱️

People stay longer

Bingo, reactions and awards keep the room playing between their own songs.

🔁

Regulars come back

Weekly streaks, records and a calendar reminder bring the same faces back every week.

🎛️

Host tools

A tip menu, secret songs, song requests, a crowd applause meter and moderation from your phone.

💻

Works with your setup

A small helper on the host’s karaoke laptop sends each song as it starts. Nothing changes for the singers.

How a bar gets set up

  1. Your bar gets its own pageokenight.com/yourbar, with your night’s schedule and your host’s name.
  2. The host installs the helperIt runs quietly on the karaoke laptop and sends each song to your page.
  3. Put the code on the tables and the TVGuests scan it and play along. That’s it.
