        Take a singer off the site

        When someone asks (at the bar, or by email to info@synthwaveweb.com), type their singer name exactly as the site shows it. Their songs stay in the night’s totals, but the name becomes “Removed singer” and a number everywhere on this bar’s pages, including songs they sing later. This can’t be undone. Privacy page

        Songbook

        Crowd meter mic

        Scores the applause after each song. The karaoke laptop listens on its own when its sync is running. This phone can listen too, as a backup or a second spot: park it at the host table, away from the speakers, and leave this page open. Only loudness numbers are sent, never sound.
